## SECTION 7 — How It Works (User Journey)

### Section Label (eyebrow)

> How it works

### Headline

> From first lead to paid invoice — the full loop in one place.

### Steps

**Step 1 — A lead finds your portfolio**
> Your public portfolio page lives at trypofolio.com/portfolio (or your own domain). They browse your work, read reviews, and fill out the Discovery Form.

**Step 2 — You convert the inquiry**
> The inquiry lands in your inbox with everything you need: project type, budget range, timeline, feature requirements, and their contact info. Convert them to a client record with one click.

**Step 3 — You run the project**
> Create a project, add services with pricing, and track status. Store their website credentials in the Vault as you go.

**Step 4 — You send the invoice**
> Open the invoice form, select the project — line items fill in automatically. Choose a template, hit download or share. Client gets a link they can open without logging in.

**Step 5 — You get paid**
> Mark the invoice Paid. Ask for a review. The testimonial appears on your portfolio. The cycle closes.

## SECTION 10 — FAQ

### Section Headline

> Common questions

---

**Q: Who is Pofolio built for?**
> Freelance web designers, developers, graphic designers, brand designers, and small creative agencies (1–5 people). Anyone who sends invoices, manages a client roster, and wants a portfolio that reflects their actual work.

**Q: Does Pofolio work offline?**
> Yes. All data saves to your browser's local storage. When you go back online, it syncs to the cloud automatically.

**Q: How does the public portfolio work?**
> Your portfolio page is built from the data in your Pofolio account — your completed projects, client reviews, services, process, FAQ, and company logos. You control everything from your Profile settings. The page is public and requires no login for visitors.

**Q: How does invoice sharing work?**
> Each invoice gets a unique shareable URL. The invoice data is encoded directly in that URL — no server needed. Send it to your client, their accountant, anyone. They open it in a browser without creating an account.

**Q: Does Pofolio support international invoicing?**
> Yes. Pofolio supports 12 currencies and 12 optional international compliance fields — including IBAN, Incoterms® 2020, HS codes, Tax ID / VAT numbers, Governing Law, Retention of Title, and Country of Origin. Toggle them on globally in Settings or per individual invoice.

**Q: Can I store multiple bank accounts?**
> Yes. Add as many payment accounts as you need — each with bank name, account number, IBAN, SWIFT/routing, and branch. Select which account appears on each invoice.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
> All data is stored in Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security enabled. Your data is strictly isolated — no other user can read or write your records.
